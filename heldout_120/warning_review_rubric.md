Each case is scored at notice level. Any nonempty checker warning counts as a flag. A clean notice with a warning is a false alarm, and an incorrect notice without a warning is missed.

For flagged incorrect notices, compare the warning text and the reported bilingual passages with the approved rationale:

- **Specific** — the warning describes the changed fact or relationship, or names differing values that unambiguously identify it in the reported passages.
- **Generic** — the warning points to the passage with the defect but only indicates possible contradiction, low similarity, a broad negation difference, or uncertain alignment.
- **Unrelated** — the checker flags another passage or another feature without identifying the planted defect.

When a notice has several warnings, assign the highest supported rating. This is a manual review of the checker output against the approved labels.
