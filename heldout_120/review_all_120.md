# English–Cantonese notice checker: 120 cases for approval

The English is the source. The Chinese uses Hong Kong Traditional Chinese public-notice wording. Each issue row has a deliberate change in meaning. The examples are synthetic. Difficulty labels are editorial estimates. Each difficulty tier contains 20 clean and 20 incorrect notices. Checker execution awaits approval.
